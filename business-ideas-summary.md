# Business Ideas - Quick Reference

A one-liner summary of each business idea.

---

## Original Ideas (1-23)

1. **TikTok Shop Analytics and Profit Dashboard** - Dashboard showing true profit margins for TikTok Shop sellers after fees, shipping, and costs.

2. **Time Tracking with Auto Invoice Generation** - Freelancer tool that tracks billable hours and automatically generates invoices from logged time.

3. **API Documentation Auto-Sync Tool** - Automatically keeps API documentation in sync when the codebase changes.

4. **E-commerce Multi-Channel Inventory Sync** - Syncs inventory quantities across multiple selling platforms to prevent overselling.

5. **Photography Client Follow-Up Automation (CRM)** - Automated email/text sequences for photographers from inquiry through rebooking.

6. **Smart Scheduling for Any Weather-Dependent Business** - Auto-reschedules appointments when weather forecasts are unfavorable and notifies customers.

7. **Receipt to Expense Automation** - Snap receipt photos, auto-extract and categorize expenses for accounting/taxes.

8. **Multi-Platform Creator Revenue Tracker** - Unified dashboard aggregating creator income from YouTube, Patreon, sponsorships, merch, etc.

9. **Voice Assistant AI (Niche Applications)** - Customizable voice AI built for specific industry workflows where hands-free matters.

10. **Third-Party Apps for Facebook Marketplace** - Power tools for resellers: price alerts, arbitrage finder, listing analytics, bulk management.

11. **AI Video Generator for Viral Character Clips** - Generate short-form video content with AI characters for social media.

12. **Bible Note Taker and Prayer Recorder** - Organized note-taking for sermons, Bible study, and prayer journals with scripture tagging.

13. **AI Home Decor / Interior Visualization App** - See how furniture, paint, or decor would look in your space using AI.

14. **Emoji/Sticker Creator Tool (MojiLab-Style)** - Create custom emoji and sticker packs from photos or AI generation.

15. **Logo Maker for Specific Niches** - AI logo generator specialized for specific industries (food trucks, podcasts, gyms, etc.).

16. **Food Delivery Health Analyzer** - Browser extension adding health scores and dietary filters to DoorDash, UberEats, etc.

17. **Write Where You Want, Publish Everywhere** - Write in Notion/Google Docs/Obsidian, auto-publish to blog, Medium, LinkedIn, newsletter.

18. **DJ Booking Service with Playlist Management** - Book DJs with integrated "must play" and "do not play" list management.

19. **Expert Clone Service** - AI trained on an expert's books/courses/content that can answer questions in their voice 24/7.

20. **Content Repurposing Automation** - Transform one piece of content into tweets, LinkedIn posts, carousels, shorts, and more.

21. **Bluesky Creator Analytics** - Analytics dashboard for creators on Bluesky (follower growth, engagement, best times).

22. **Resale Marketplace Cross-Listing Automation** - List items once, publish to eBay/Poshmark/Mercari/etc., auto-delist when sold.

23. **Whatnot Livestream Seller Tools** - Inventory management, live show dashboards, and analytics for Whatnot sellers.
