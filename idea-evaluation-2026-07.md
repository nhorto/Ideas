# Idea Evaluation — July 2026 Update

Six months after the original list (January 2026), this is a re-evaluation of all 36 ideas
against what's changed in the market, plus a recommendation on which to pursue.

## What changed since January

- **TikTok resolved (mostly).** TikTok's US operations were divested into "TikTok USDS"
  (Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX consortium) on Jan 22, 2026. TikTok Shop is operational and open
  to new sellers. The existential risk that hung over ideas #1 and #23's ecosystem has
  largely cleared, though platform policy churn continues (e.g., July 2026 ban on AI voices
  in live commerce, new Account Health Rating system).
- **Live shopping went mainstream.** Whatnot sellers did **$8B in GMV in 2025 (2x YoY)**;
  Whatnot holds ~60% of a ~$22B US/EU live-shopping market. 1 in 8 Whatnot sellers is now
  full-time (+20% YoY), and daily sellers average ~$69k/month in sales. This is the single
  biggest positive market shift for any idea on the list (#23).
- **Bluesky plateaued.** ~43.5M registered users but only ~1.5–3M daily actives, with growth
  stabilized at 2–3%. The creator monetization base for #21 never materialized at scale.
- **AI receptionist/concierge exploded — and got crowded.** Voice AI for SMBs is one of the
  hottest categories of 2026 (Retell AI at $40M+ ARR, dozens of funded competitors at
  $49–300/mo price points). #33's thesis was right, but the generic version of it is now a
  red ocean. Only a tightly verticalized wedge is viable.
- **Generative AI commoditized several ideas.** One-shot logo generation (#15), sticker/emoji
  creation (#14), receipt OCR (#7), viral AI video (#11), and generic content repurposing
  (#20) are now largely solved by frontier models or saturated app categories. These are
  features, not companies.
- **This repo gained a real asset.** The Holistic Bravo recipe extraction (45 recipes,
  Feb 2026) means there's an actual content business in motion — a vegetarian/high-protein
  food blog with a YouTube source channel. That changes the founder-fit math: ideas that
  compound with that audience get a boost.

## Evaluation criteria

Each idea scored 1–5 on: **Market timing** (is the window open now?), **Competition**
(can a solo builder win a niche?), **Willingness to pay** (do buyers spend money here?),
**Solo-buildable** (scope realistic for one person + AI tooling?), and **Founder fit**
(does it compound with what's already in motion — the food/content business?).

## Recommendation

**Primary: #23 — Whatnot seller tools.** It has the best combination of market momentum
(verifiable, dramatic growth), buyers with money and pain, and a scope where a solo builder
can ship a wedge (start with post-show analytics + shipping batch automation — the two
things sellers complain about most) in weeks, not months. Main risk: platform dependency.
Mitigate by expanding to TikTok Live / eBay Live sellers once the wedge works.

**Secondary (parallel, low cost): the food cluster.** Keep building Holistic Bravo — it's
the only idea here with distribution already in motion. Automate the YouTube→blog pipeline
(internal use of #20), grow the audience, and let the audience tell you which food/health
tool (#30-lite, meal planner, macro tools) to build. Content compounds while #23 is being
validated.

**Watchlist: #36 (local business monitoring)** — build it if/when #23 stalls; it's the best
"boring but durable" fallback on the list.

**Explicitly dropped:** #21, #15, #14, #7, #11, #19 (as products), #13 consumer, #2/#32.
